summary: a mockup bead waits here until its brief is ready to design from

A mockup bead asks for three design directions to pick from, not for a page to
ship. Before it is sent to `mockups` its body says:

- the subject: what the product, place or person is, in one concrete sentence;
- the audience: who comes to the page;
- the primary job: the one thing the page gets a visitor to do;
- the page: which page of the site, or which screen of the app, the three
  mockups show;
- reference sites: addresses of sites the person likes or dislikes, each with
  what to take from it or leave;
- what the brief fixes: brand colours, typefaces, a logo, copy that must
  appear, or that nothing is fixed;
- what is not wanted: looks already tried and turned down.

Send it with `yardr bead advance <id> --to mockups`.
