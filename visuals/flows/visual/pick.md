summary: a person looks at the shots, names the direction to hold, or sends the bead back

For a person. What to read: the designer's note on the bead, which carries the
three shots at the brief's exact size and names each direction
(`yardr prime --bead <id>` prints the bead with every note in full). The
directions are files that open on their own, on the bead's branch `yard/<bead>`
under `directions/`; the addresses on the designer's note stay open while the
bead is here, and the shots are what to judge them by.

A bead leaves `pick` when a person moves it, with words:

- to `picked` when a direction is taken: the bead closes, and nothing lands on
  the base. Name the direction in your words, by its number and name, and say
  what to keep from the others. What you hold is the picked file with its PNG
  at the exact size (and its PDF where the brief is for print), on the branch
  under `directions/`;
- to `directions` with a note when none is there yet: say what to change, and
  the designer composes three again;
- to `backlog` to park it or have the brief reshaped.

A bead also comes here when the designer could not start without an answer: the
note says what it needs. Answer in a note and send it back to `directions`.

When a bead arrives the yard puts a card for these moves on it, and picking one
moves the bead: your words with the pick stay on the bead for whoever works it
next. A bead with an ask already open gets no card, and a card gives way to an
ask put after it: that question is what you answer, and the bead is moved by
hand once it is.

## Ask
kind: moves
question: Which direction do you hold, or where should this bead go next?
context:
- The designer's note holds the three shots at the brief's exact size and names each direction. The directions are on the bead's branch under directions/.
- To hold one, move the bead to picked and name it in your words. To ask for another round, move it back to directions and say what to change.
- Your words with the pick stay on the bead for whoever works it next.
