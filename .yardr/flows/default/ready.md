Folded carriers wait here for a person to send them to build. No agent works this stage.

A bead here is one carrier: several small changes folded into one, to be built and reviewed in one round. The fold is automatic; the build is not: the carrier waits here for a person's say, for quota.

The stage has no outcomes; the yardmaster (or mckean) moves the bead (`yardr bead advance <id> --to <stage>`):

- to `new`, to build the bundle;
- to `backlog`, when the bundle must be reshaped or split (a part taken out with `yardr bead unfold` reopens there on its own).
