Shaped small beads wait here for company, and leave folded into one bead. No agent works this stage.

A bead here is shaped and ready; it waits for company, not for shaping. The yard folds the waiting beads into one carrier bead by itself, when enough of them wait or long enough after the latest arrival, and the carrier starts in `ready`; `yardr flow show yardr-packs` says the numbers. A bead that cannot move (held, claimed, blocked, or with a question open) stays where it is and is named in the fold.

The stage has no outcomes; the yardmaster (or mckean) moves a bead out by hand only to take it out of the siding (`yardr bead advance <id> --to backlog`): when it needs reshaping, when it is held, or when the siding refused to fold for weighing too much and some part must go alone.
